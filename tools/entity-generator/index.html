Entity Generator

An entity is the outside view of a VHDL block: its name and its ports (the pins that carry signals in and out). Fill in the form and copy the ready-made VHDL.

1. Name your block

The block's name, e.g. counter, alu, uart_tx.

Names the inside (the logic) of the block. rtl or behavioral are common.

2. Add its ports

Tips: modes, types and width
Mode: which way the signal flows
in: comes into the block. out: goes out of the block. inout: both ways, e.g. a shared data bus. buffer: an output the block can also read back.
Type: what the port carries
std_logic: one wire. std_logic_vector: a group of wires (a bus). unsigned / signed: a bus treated as a number you can do maths with (signed can be negative).
Width: how many wires a bus has
8 gives (7 downto 0): 8 wires numbered 7 to 0, where bit 7 is the most significant bit (the one worth the most).
